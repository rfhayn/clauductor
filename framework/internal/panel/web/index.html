How to

  • Start a lane: New lane, pick a template or a lane type, name it, then Start lane.
  • Type into a lane: Enter or click on its terminal; Ctrl+] leaves.
  • Copy: drag to select, then ⌘C. Open a link: ⌘-click it.
  • Give claude an image: drop it on the terminal, or paste it with ⌘V.
  • Stop lane ends claude and keeps the worktree; Close lane also removes the worktree and branch when nothing would be lost. The ⋯ on a lane's row has both, and asks first.
  • After a reboot: Restore all, in the Restore bar, resumes every lane that lost its session.
  • How the work flows and what it costs: Metrics, next to Activity. Each figure says whether the project's metrics command or the panel gave it, and "—" says why there is none.
  • Another repository: clauductor panel init, then trust, then add, and restart the panel.
