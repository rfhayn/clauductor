## What happened

`TestGateStopsATimedOutClaudeWithSIGTERM` (#314) and two sibling tests used one fake shape: a TERM trap, `sleep 60 &`, "ready", then `wait $P`. Under macOS's sh (bash 3.2) it loses the signal in two ways. If the SIGTERM lands after "ready" but before `wait` blocks, the trap stays pending until the child exits: no TERM mark, which is #314's failure. And the trap's `kill $P` can reach the forked child before it execs `sleep` and be swallowed by the trap the child inherited. The child then holds the call's output open, and the call ends at the 15 s grace. A probe that sends TERM at "ready" found the first race in 2–5% of runs and the second in 16%. Under load (24–32 concurrent `-race` copies beside 12 busy loops, load average 33–52), the three tests failed 5 times in 1200 runs before the fix and 0 in 1008 after. `trapsTERM` replaces the shape with a trap, "ready", then a loop of `sleep 0.1`.

## Decisions

- Fix the fakes, not the gate: the gate's SIGTERM path (its signal delivery) was not at fault. The probe reproduced both races with a direct SIGTERM and no gate at all.
- Round 1 found a medium in older code: a stopped claude's child that holds the output is orphaned, and its call ends only at the grace. The lead deferred it to #318 and row OPS-159 rather than widen this fix.

## Surprises

- The first reproduction under load failed at a different assertion than #314's (killed at 15 s, though the TERM mark was there). That pointed to the second race.
- `ps` is blocked in the lane's sandbox, so `TestWatchLogsALockHeldByAnotherProcess` fails there before and after the change. It is not a flake.
