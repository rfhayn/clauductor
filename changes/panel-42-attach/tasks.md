## 3. Commands stay in the repository
- [ ] 3.1 A pure function in `framework/internal/panel/config` lists every command word in a config that resolves outside a root, by design.md D5's rule. The words come from argv; for `sh`, `bash`, `zsh` or `dash` with `-c`, also from its script, split on whitespace and `;` `&&` `||` `|` `(` `)`, with quotes removed and a leading redirection dropped. The part after `=` counts as well. It refuses `~`, `$` and backticks, absolute paths, and paths that leave the root when joined to it and resolved through symlinks. Bare words are allowed. Tested in a new `framework/internal/panel/config/outside_test.go` (a table: each refused shape, each allowed shape, a symlink out, a symlink that stays in) citing [ATTACH-3-S3] and [ATTACH-3-S5]
- [ ] 3.2 `install.CheckTrust` and `install.TrustedNow` refuse a config outside its repository whose list is not empty: no record is written, and an entry recorded earlier does not count. The trust report (`TrustConfigReport` and `PrintTrusted`, `Inspect`'s `Runs`, the page's `trustReport`) marks each offending line `OUTSIDE THE REPOSITORY (<word>): …`, and `panel trust` prints them and exits non-zero. Tested in `framework/internal/panel/install/trust_test.go` citing [ATTACH-3-S1], [ATTACH-3-S3], [ATTACH-3-S4] and [ATTACH-3-S5], and in `framework/internal/panel/live_integration_test.go` citing [ATTACH-3-S2] (a hand-written trust entry; the project loads untrusted) and [ATTACH-3-S6] (the `/api/projects/{id}/trust` report and the trust, refused).
