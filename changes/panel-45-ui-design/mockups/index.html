The panel's new look: three terminal models of one direction

One direction, the owner's (D2): a modern developer tool, with mission control's terminal as a first-class pane. The three variants share everything (colour carries state, rounded panes, ⌘K, the sidebar, Pulse) and differ only in how the terminal comes and goes. Each draws the real harness data, states its rules (press R, or the Rules button), and follows the system's light or dark mode unless the sidebar's switch overrides it.
