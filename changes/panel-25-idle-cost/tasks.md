- [ ] 3.2 `Ticks.MergedLanes` (3 min) in `DefaultTicks` and `withDefaults`. `pollAutoClose` keeps its `r.lanes != nil && r.trusted()` gate and its watched-lane filter; with a watched lane it runs the merged-list read, `gh pr list --state merged --search "merged:>=<since>" --limit 100 --json number,headRefName,mergedAt`, built by a new `signals.MergedSinceArgv(since time.Time)` beside `signals.MergedArgv` (left as it is: a date, limit 300, the Metrics view's fields, pinned by `TestParseMergedPRsRealShape`), at most every `MergedLanes`, in view or not; `<since>` is RFC 3339 in UTC with `Z` (`time.RFC3339` of the UTC time, never a `+` offset), the last good read's start minus 10 min (the panel's start minus 10 min at first), and a failed read leaves it put; a lane whose branch is in the list under an unchecked number is due; with no watched lane it runs nothing. `MergedSinceArgv`'s shape and its `Z` are pinned in `framework/internal/panel/signals/merged_test.go`. The decision tested in `idle_test.go` with a fake `gh` that sorts its merged rows newest-created first, filters them by the `merged:>=` it parses from the argv's `--search` (none when absent), and applies `--limit`. Proved by the falsifying edit before it lands: with `--search` dropped from `MergedSinceArgv` the fake returns the 100 newest-created rows, the lane's PR not among them, and the test fails.
